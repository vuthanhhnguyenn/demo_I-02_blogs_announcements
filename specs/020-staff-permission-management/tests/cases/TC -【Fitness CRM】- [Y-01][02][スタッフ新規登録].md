# TC -【Fitness CRM】- [Y-01-01][スタッフ新規登録] (Staff Registration — bulk)

**Screen**: スタッフ新規登録（一括） (Staff Registration, bulk) — `StaffForm` (`mode="create"`)
**Route**: `/staffs/create`
**Unit**: #2 of `specs/020-staff-permission-management/tests/test-plan.md`
**Scope**: the create-mode form only (multi-row batch registration + shared role/position/affiliation).
Out of scope (per plan): 職位マスター管理 (position-master CRUD), 職位別権限プレビュー, CSV出力権限, and the Staff Edit / Staff Detail / Staff List screens (separate TC docs).
