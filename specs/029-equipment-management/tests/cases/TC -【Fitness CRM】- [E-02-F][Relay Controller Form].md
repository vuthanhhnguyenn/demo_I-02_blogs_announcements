# TC -【Fitness CRM】- [E-02-F][Relay Controller Form]

**Unit**: E-02-F Controller Form (per [../test-plan.md](../test-plan.md), unit 6 of 6 — **executed first** per the plan's §9 order, because it creates the `QA-E02-CTRL-*` fixtures every other unit uses)
**Screen**: 接点制御装置 新規登録／編集 (Relay controller register / edit) — one form, two modes, routes `/controllers/create` and `/controllers/[id]/edit`
**Scope**: every field and its client- and server-side validation, both modes (prefill, partial update, optimistic lock), the discard dialog, role gating incl. direct URLs, store scope of the 店舗コード picker, session expiry, persistence, and layout under stress values at 1024px.
**Out of scope** (per the test plan): the controller list (only that a save lands there and the new / changed row is visible), the controller detail (only its 編集 entry point and read-back of saved values), the ステータス変更 / 削除 dialogs and the 変更履歴 tab (unit E-02-E), controller CSV (unit E-02-D), connected-device screens (units A–C), FR-013.
