# TC -【Fitness CRM】- [E-02-C][Connected Equipment Form]

**Unit**: E-02-C Equipment Form (per [../test-plan.md](../test-plan.md), unit 3 of 6 — executed **third**, after E-02-F and E-02-D, per §9)
**Screen**: 接続機器 新規登録 / 接続機器 編集 (Connected Equipment Create / Edit) — one form in two modes, routes `/equipment/create` and `/equipment/[id]/edit`, plus the 離脱確認 (discard) dialog
**Scope**: every field of 基本情報 / 接続情報 (incl. 出力ポート) / 利用制御ルール / 備考, validation, the controller picker, create/edit against the real API, role gating and store scope, the unsaved-changes guard, layout under stress values. Since 2026-11-19 also 機器タイプ エリア扉 (`[エリア扉]` cases TC-144…TC-169).
**Out of scope** (per test plan §1/§2): the list screen (only that create/edit lands there and the new row appears), the detail screen (only read-back of saved values), status-change and delete dialogs (unit B), controller screens (units D/E/F — used only to create fixtures and check 紐付き機器数), FR-013, header store switcher as a feature.
