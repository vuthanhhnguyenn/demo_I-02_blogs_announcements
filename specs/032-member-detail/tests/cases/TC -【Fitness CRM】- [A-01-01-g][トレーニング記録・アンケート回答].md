# TC -【Fitness CRM】- [A-01-01-g][トレーニング記録・アンケート回答]

**Scope (Unit 8 of the [test plan](../test-plan.md))** — two read-only tabs of the member detail screen (`/members/{memberId}`): **アンケート回答** (`?tab=survey` — the member's survey-response list, `G-04 FR-004` 個別回答参照) and **トレーニング記録** (`?tab=training`, `A-01-01-g` — the training-record table with its period filter and the 記録サマリー card). Covers the tab entry / URL sync for these two tabs, every element and state of both tabs, the role and store-scope gates, the real-API baseline (401, out-of-scope, malformed id, persistence), and layout at the 1024px floor.

**Out of scope for this document** — the survey response **detail** screen and the G-04 回答データ一覧 / 集計 / CSV screens (link intent only); the other eight tabs and the head-up card (Units 1, 4–7, 9, 10); training data entry (the mobile app, Y-08 / Y-09, is the system of record). Neither tab has a write path, so this document creates **no** test data.
