# TC -【Fitness CRM】- [A-01-01-a][アプリ連携]

**Scope (Unit 11 of the [test plan](../test-plan.md))** — the アプリ連携 (app link) surface of the member detail screen (`/members/{memberId}`, head-up `A-01-01-a`, `A-01 FR-027`): the 4th summary-strip item アプリ連携 with its state and tooltip, the アプリ連携 sheet (今の状態, 行う操作, account search, identity check, footer), its three confirm dialogs (紐付け / 紐付け直し / 連携解除), ロック解除 from the sheet, the success toast with 「履歴を見る」, the 変更履歴 rows the four writes leave, server refusals, the role gates (UI and API) and the layout at 1024px.

**Out of scope for this document** — the rest of the head-up and the other three strip items (Unit 1); the 変更履歴 tab's own mechanics (Unit 10 — only the app-link rows are read here); the mobile app's own link flow and lock counting (APP-F-02 — locks are built as fixtures); 本部による遠隔でのロック解除 (A-01 FR-027: 対象外).
