# TC -【Fitness CRM】- [A-01-01-h][ボディーデータ]

**Scope (Unit 9 of the [test plan](../test-plan.md))** — the **ボディーデータ** tab of the member detail screen (`/members/{memberId}?tab=body-data`): the consent gate (granted / 未同意 / 拒否) and its network-level privacy guarantee, 最新記録サマリー, 体組成データ (12 metrics), 身体データ (block 2 with separate left/right values + 身長), the metric-switchable 推移グラフ, 測定履歴 (latest 5 + 「全{n}件」) and its full-history side sheet. Includes the loading / error / empty states, role and data-scope behaviour, the read-only guarantee, persistence, and 1024px layout stress.

**Out of scope for this document** — the tab bar as a whole, the head-up card and banners (Unit 1 — this doc only checks that the ボディーデータ tab is reachable and URL-synced); every other tab; **paging of the 測定履歴 full list** (on hold by PO 2026-09-16 until N44 is solved — one deferred case, TC-059); creating or editing body data (owned by the mobile app / `E-02` devices — the CRM is read-only); the member-app endpoints themselves.

## Fixture register — fill in at execution, before any case

The consent state is read from `GET /admin/members/{id}` (`bodyDataCrmConsentAt` non-null = granted).
