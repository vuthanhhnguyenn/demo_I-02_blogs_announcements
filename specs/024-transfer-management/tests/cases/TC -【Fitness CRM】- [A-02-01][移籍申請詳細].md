# TC -【Fitness CRM】- [A-02-01][移籍申請詳細]

**Screen**: Transfer Request Detail (移籍申請詳細) — `/members/transfers/[id]`
**Unit**: Unit 2 of [test-plan.md](../test-plan.md)
**Scope**: The transfer detail — back link, title + status badge, member head-up card, JOYFIT exclusion alerts, 移籍情報 card, 承認フロー timeline (JOYFIT 3 / FIT365 4 steps), sticky status card with 承認 / 否認, and the three dialogs that live only inside this screen (承認, 否認, 手動解除).
**Out of scope** (per test plan §1): the transfer list (Unit 1 — used only to reach a row and to confirm list/detail agreement), A-01-01 member detail (only "the link opens it" and the contracted store it shows), A-01 member form (data setup only), notifications (copy only), the system-actor scheduling mechanism.
