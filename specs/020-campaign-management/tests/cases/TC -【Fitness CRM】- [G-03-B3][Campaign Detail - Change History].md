**Data limitations** (per test-plan seed-reset note: "Before Unit 4, need at least one campaign with an empty history and one with several entries"):

- A campaign with **zero** history rows is required for TC-010 and does not exist in the mockup's hardcoded sample — must be seeded/confirmed at execution.
- A campaign with **exactly one** entry (creation only, never edited) is required for TC-009 — not in the sample, confirm at execution.
- No sample row exercises a **before-value badge** (`fromBadge`) — only plain text or dash appears in the before column of the given data (TC-007 flags this explicitly).
- Long-string, full-width/half-width, XSS-payload, and large-volume (50+ row) entries (TC-014–TC-017, TC-021) are not in the sample dataset; they must be seeded or substituted with the nearest available data at execution, per each case's Notes.
