# TC -【Fitness CRM】- [A-03][休会・退会管理（月別ビュー）] (Leave & Withdrawal Management — Monthly view)

**Scope**: The 月別ビュー (monthly view) of the suspension/withdrawal screen — route `/members/leaves` with 月別ビュー selected in the toolbar toggle (PO spec `A-03` FR-001 「月別ビュー・休会退会履歴」). Covers the 一覧 / 月別ビュー toggle and its URL persistence, the member × 7-month grid, the member cell, month-cell badges and overlap priority, the month tooltip and click-through, the 月別ビュー-only 表示 filter, whole-set evaluation across pages, empty / loading / error states, role spot-checks and layout under stress.

**Out of scope** (navigation intent only, never the destination): the 一覧 table, its row ⋯ menu, pagination and the 退会取り消し dialog (Unit 1 — `TC -【Fitness CRM】- [A-03][休会・退会管理（一覧）].md`); the application detail `/members/leaves/[id]` (Unit 3); member detail A-01-01; the header store-scope selector and 「デモ: ユーザー切替」 (preconditions only). **Shared filter mechanics** (each option list, panel toggle, highlight, banner accounting, 予定日 ranges) are owned by Unit 1 — here only one value per filter is spot-checked to prove it drives the grid, plus the 表示 filter in full.

Driven by [test-plan.md](../test-plan.md) — Unit 2 (Monthly view), §3 row 2, §5 Unit 2. Regenerated from scratch on 2026-10-06 (no case carried over from the 2026-08 docs, which asserted 月別ビュー absent — reversed by Q-17).
