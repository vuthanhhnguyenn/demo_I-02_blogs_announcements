# TC -【Fitness CRM】- [G-04-F][Survey Form]

**Scope**: unit **U2 — Survey form** of `specs/033-survey-management` (test plan §2): `/surveys/create` (アンケート新規登録) and `/surveys/[id]/edit` (アンケート編集). One form component, so this doc covers both modes, plus the two delete-question dialogs (この設問を削除しますか？ / この設問を削除できません) and the discard dialog (変更を破棄しますか？). Saves: `POST /admin/questionnaires`, `PUT /admin/questionnaires/{id}` and `PATCH …/status`; the trigger master `GET /admin/questionnaire-triggers`.
**Out of scope here** (other units or specs): list mechanics and the 新規登録 button gate (U1), detail screen and disable dialog (U3), store visibility screen (U4, used here only to set up TC-075), responses and analytics (U5, U6), copy and change history (no CRM UI), and the member-app answer flow. Phase 2 items (オペレーション creation, 配信設定, 評価 / NPS, 手動配信 / 条件トリガー) are checked for **absence** only.
