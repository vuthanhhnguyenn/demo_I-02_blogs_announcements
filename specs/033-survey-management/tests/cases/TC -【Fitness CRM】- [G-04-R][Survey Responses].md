# TC -【Fitness CRM】- [G-04-R][Survey Responses]

**Unit**: U5 — Responses, per [`../test-plan.md`](../test-plan.md) §2–§5 (response list + response detail, clustered: the detail is only reachable from a list row and needs the list's `questionnaireId`).
**Screens**: `/surveys/responses` (回答データ一覧 — Response list) and `/surveys/responses/[responseId]?questionnaireId=<id>` (個別回答詳細 — Response detail).
**Scope**: entry points and back links, the response table and its derived cells (回答日時, 状態, ブランド), filters (date range, ブランド, 店舗, 会員区分, テンプレート種別, search), filter count / highlight / result banner / clear, empty states, pagination, CSV 出力 (content, BOM, filename, permission), role × store scope (UI and server), unknown / missing ids, 401, loading / error states, the detail's header, basic info, status card and answers, and long-value layout at 1024×768.
**Out of scope** (plan §1): the survey list / form / detail / visibility / analytics screens (U1–U4, U6 — used here only as entry points), the member detail アンケート回答 tab (`032-member-detail`), the member app answering a survey (the only way answers are created), Phase 2 items (FR-005, FR-006, FR-S001, FR-S003, FR-S004), header store selector and sidebar themselves.
