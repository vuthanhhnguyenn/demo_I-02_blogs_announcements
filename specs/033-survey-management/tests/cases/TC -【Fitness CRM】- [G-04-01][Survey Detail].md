**Data limitations**

- **L1** — Every disable case needs an **有効** QA-SVY survey, which needs a trigger T that is free for its brand (no 有効 survey with an intersecting brand scope on T; `E-QST-010` is per trigger and brand scope, API-090 §7.3). If every trigger is occupied for the needed brand by pre-existing surveys, the disable cases are `Block — data limitation (no free trigger)`. On the shared dev BE, activating a test survey is not allowed (test-plan §9) → the same `Block`.
- **L2** — PX-COUNSELING / PX-MULTIBRAND / PX-RAWCODE cannot be created from the CRM; if absent, their cases are `Block — data limitation`.
- **L3** — Answer-derived values (回答サマリー, 最終回答) need answers from the member app → `B10`.
- **L4** — FX-VIS / FX-VIS-EMPTY depend on the U4 toggles; restore every toggle to ON at the end (test-plan §9).
