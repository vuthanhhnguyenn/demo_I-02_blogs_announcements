# TC -【Fitness CRM】- [Y-03-A][FC企業一覧]

**Scope**: Unit 1 of [test-plan.md](../test-plan.md) covers FC企業一覧 `/franchise-companies`. **Out of scope** (the plan puts these in other units): the create/edit form (Y-03-C, only its entry points are checked here), detail display, status changes and the detail delete (Y-03-B1), managed stores and history (Y-03-B2). TC-063 links a store only to stage the delete race. The link flow itself is tested in Y-03-B2.
