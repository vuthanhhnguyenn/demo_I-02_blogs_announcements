# TC -【Fitness CRM】- [Y-03-B2][FC企業詳細 管轄店舗・変更履歴]

**Scope**: Unit 3 of [test-plan.md](../test-plan.md). It covers two tabs of the FC企業詳細 screen `/franchise-companies/[id]`:

- The **管轄店舗** tab: the table, empty state, 店舗を紐づけ dialog (search, candidates, 「現在: 〇〇」), 付け替え confirmation, 解除 dialog, live fan-out of counts and states, two-tab conflicts, inactive-company rule and role gating.
- The **変更履歴** tab: every line produced by registration, edits, status changes, links and unlinks, plus ordering, JST timestamps, the operator, persistence, and layout under stress.

**Out of scope** (these belong to other units in the plan):

- The header, 基本情報, StatusCard and delete flow (Y-03-B1). This unit only reads their state after a link or unlink.
- The store detail screen `/stores/[id]` (Y-02, still on the mock; P7).
- Setting the operating company from the store side (Y-02).
