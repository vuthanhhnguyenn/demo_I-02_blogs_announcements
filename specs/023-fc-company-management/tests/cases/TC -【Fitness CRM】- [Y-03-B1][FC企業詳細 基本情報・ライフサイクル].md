# TC -【Fitness CRM】- [Y-03-B1][FC企業詳細 基本情報・ライフサイクル]

**Scope**: Unit 2 of [test-plan.md](../test-plan.md). It covers the FC企業詳細 screen `/franchise-companies/[id]`: the header (back link, title, status and 区分 badges, 削除 / 編集), the tab bar (including the Phase 2 集計 tab), the 基本情報 tab, the StatusCard, the 無効化 / 有効化 dialogs, the delete flow with its server guard, the not-found / loading / error / 401 states, role access, and layout under stress.
**Out of scope** (these belong to other units in the plan): the 管轄店舗 tab and its link/unlink dialogs, and the assertions on the 変更履歴 tab (Y-03-B2). Two cases here, TC-028 and TC-046, look at the stores tab only to confirm a state produced on this screen. The history lines that 無効化 / 有効化 produce are asserted in Y-03-B2, except the B3 reason check in TC-029. The form is Y-03-C and the list is Y-03-A.
