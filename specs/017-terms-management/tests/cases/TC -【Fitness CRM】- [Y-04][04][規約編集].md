## Known Round 1 gaps

None. `report.md` shows every row `OK / OK`, so there is no `Bn` table. Plan gaps touching this unit: **G1** (rows cannot be removed - extra throw-aways are residue), **G3** (statuses are checked with constructed dates), **G4/G6** (non-HQ brand group undocumented), **G5** (published fixtures reach real dev-app users - FX1 after the date change, FX5, FX7 are live rows; toggle stays OFF, delete early).

**Environment limits that can turn cases into Block (record the failing request, not NG)**:

- **Presign quota**: every valid PDF selection creates a pending presign session; the backend allows **5 per user** (`429 E-MED-008`). This document needs at most **2 sessions on PC-A** (TC-058 valid replacement, TC-059 cancel-replacement) and **3 on PC-B** (TC-060 replace twice = 2, TC-061 image-only = 1; TC-062 deliberately exceeds the quota). Run the PDF cases **first in a fresh session / after earlier sessions expired**, and count the selections made in Unit 3 for the same user. Invalid files (wrong type, over 10 MB) are rejected client-side and consume none.
- **Storage stub**: the local backend's presign URL is a non-resolvable stub, so the storage PUT cannot complete. Cases that must persist a replaced PDF (valid replacement, replace twice, image-only) may come back **Block**; the form-side steps (preview, card, request order) are still judged.
