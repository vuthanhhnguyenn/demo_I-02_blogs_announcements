# TC -【Fitness CRM】- [Y-02-03C][店舗詳細 契約・料金 プラン・オプション・キャンペーン]

**Scope**: Store detail (`/stores/[id]`), 契約・料金 (Contract & pricing) tab — the 責務説明 banner, the **主契約プラン** (main-contract plans), **オプション** (options) and **キャンペーン** (campaigns) cards, their three 紐づけ追加 (add-link) Sheets and their three 紐づけ解除 (unlink) dialogs. Test plan unit **5**.

**Out of scope (other units / by design)**:

- 1DayPass価格設定 card, pricing Drawer and clear dialog → unit 6 (`[Y-02-03D]`). Only its position under the キャンペーン card is asserted here.
- The page shell, scope states and other tabs → units 3, 4, 7, 8.
- Authoring plans, options, campaigns, prices or discounts — done in 商材・施策 (G-01 / G-02 / G-03). Only the store-side link is tested.
- Detail FR-076 (mutual-use partner add/remove, withdrawn), FR-095 / FR-096 (mock data-source notes, no behaviour).
- Viewports narrower than 1024px.
