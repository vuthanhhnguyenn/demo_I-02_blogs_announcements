# TC -【Fitness CRM】- [Y-02-03E][店舗詳細 入退館設定]

**Scope**: Test Plan **Unit 7**. This doc covers the 入退館設定 (entry/exit settings) tab of 店舗詳細 (store detail) at `/stores/[id]`:

- the 相互利用設定 (mutual-use settings) card;
- the どこでもJOY YOGA利用料金 (JOY YOGA usage fee) card;
- the right-side Sheet 相互利用設定を編集する (edit mutual-use settings).

It covers the JOYFIT vs FIT365 brand split, the read-only partner list, the edit Sheet and its two writes (`PUT …/mutual-use`, plus the minor-entry window through `PATCH /stores/{id}`), role gating, and the long-value rows LV-07 and LV-27.

> ⛔ **Every write in this document targets a `QA-P2-` fixture store** (test plan §9). Mutual-use editing is System/HQ only, so no case writes to the Staff account's pre-existing store. Pre-existing stores are used **read-only** (R-JOY / R-SUB / R-FIT below).

**Out of scope for this document** (per the test plan):

- the page shell, the 基本情報 tab, ステータス変更 and the 変更履歴 tab (Unit 3). TC-045 reads the history row that a save in this unit produces, and nothing more;
- the 営業設定, 契約・料金, 1DayPass and 店舗マップ tabs (Units 4, 5, 6 and 8);
- **partner add/remove: former detail FR-076, withdrawn 2026-10-02 (D22).** The partner list is read-only. TC-016 asserts that no add/remove control exists;
- エリア / テリトリー (B1, Phase 2);
- viewports below 1024px.
